Sources and flow check

Flow: traced on the drawing

ESP system (flow diagram set Rev A, page 65): flue gas from the air heater outlet (PD-051) through ESP fields 1 to 5 to the ID fan; transformer-rectifiers U-1201 (power plus) and U-1202 to U-1205 (Si-TR); DE and CE rapping hammers M-1201 to M-1243; hoppers 1A to 5B with level switches, hopper vibrators and aux steam heating AS-1-740; hopper outlets to fly ash transmitters (PD-073); insulator heating fan F-1201 and heater H-1201.
Fly ash handling (pages 57 to 64): each hopper through XV-FH1700 into a 1000 litre ash transmitter (S-1250A for EP-1A), conveying air XV-PA1700, line FH-1-700 to 706 to fly ash silo T-1251 (vent fan F-1250, vent filter S-1256, LT-FH1700); dry unloader L-1250 to the truck; air heater hoppers A to D also convey to the silo.
Bottom ash (pages 53 to 55): grate floor through inlet valves XV-BH1060A to D into screw ash coolers L-1060A to D (cooling water CW-1-056 from PD-021, frequency converter speed control) to chain conveyor L-1090, bucket elevator L-1091, screw conveyor L-1092, two-way separator L-1094 (XV-BH1754); either to bottom ash silo T-1090 (bag filter S-1091, fan F-1091, LT-BH1750), outlet XV-BH1756 and loading spout L-1093, or through vibrating screen S-1092 to the recycle buffer tank T-1091 and ash transmitter S-1090 to the sand silo (PD-063).
