Sources and flow check

Flow: where it comes from

There is no turbine oil flow diagram in the repo (the K1AU3 flow-diagram set has none; it is a Hitachi supply). The flow on this page follows the plant HMI snapshot 36.PNG (036 TURBINE OIL SYSTEM): main oil tank T-1920; main oil pumps P-1922A/B and emergency oil pump P-1923 in the tank; the main oil pumps through lube oil coolers A and B (in parallel) and the oil filter (DPISL01932) to the bearing header (PILO10031 to 10033, PSL01004); the emergency oil pump line joins the header after the filter; the bearing header feeds the turbine and generator bearings and the suction of control oil pumps P-1931A/B; the control oil pumps through the control oil filter (DPISL01933A/B) to the control oil header (PILO10061 to 10063) and the main stop, control and combined reheat valves; trip oil (PILO10071 to 10073) from the main stop, control and combined reheat valves back to the master trip solenoid valves and the tank. Tank level alarms LSH-LO1920-2 / LSL-LO1920-1 (IBD; snapshot LSHL019202 / LSLL019201). Filter DP: DPISLO1932 (snapshot) and DPIS-LO1933-A / -B (IBD). Generator No.3 / No.4 bearing outlet oil temperature TEGE1007 / TEGE1008 (TCS IO list; snapshot label TIGE1007 / 1008).
Equipment numbers from the TCS logic (IBD 310SC90-794 R2): vapour extractor F-n937 (sheet TB-12), oil purifier pump P-n941 (TB-15), main oil pumps P-n922A/B (TB-1, TB-2), DC emergency oil pump P-n923 (TB-5), control oil pumps P-n931A/B (TB-21, TB-22); n = 1 for Unit 1. The purifier and coalescer filter loop back to the tank is drawn from PR-OPE-5002 section 15 and the snapshot alarm "conditioner coalescer filter diff. pressure high"; its exact piping is not in the repo.

Tags and logic

TCS IO list (iolist-tcs.zip, HMI_AI.xls): PTLO10031 to 10033 bearing oil 0 to 5, PTLO10061 to 10063 control oil 0 to 250, PTLO10071 to 10073 trip oil 0 to 250, PTLO1922A/B 0 to 10, PTLO1931A/B 0 to 250, PTLO1923 0 to 5 kg/cm2g, TELO1001 cooler inlet oil temperature, TELO10021 / 10022 cooler outlet oil temperature (A) / (B), 0 to 100 °C. HMI_DI.xls: AUTO / MANUAL / RUNNING / STOPPED / TRIPPED of P-1922A/B and P-1931A/B, PSLO1004, main oil tank level high / low, vapour extractor F-1937. DCS IO list: TCV-CW1201 (ABC-053) on the cooling water.
Logic (IBD): each main oil pump in AUTO starts when the other pump's discharge pressure is low (other running 10 s) or the other has motor trouble; TCS major trouble starts the main oil pump. The DC emergency oil pump starts on bearing oil pressure low (PS-LOn004), both main oil pumps stopped (1 s) or both in motor trouble. Each control oil pump in AUTO starts on the other's discharge pressure low (other running 15 s) or motor trouble; bearing oil pressure very low stops it. Very low control, bearing or trip oil pressure trips the turbine.

Values and NO-SOURCE

Values from snapshot 36.PNG (2023-12-01 09:27, 149.2 MW): main oil pump A 6.9, B 0.0; emergency 0.0; control oil pump A 0.0, B 164.2; bearing header 3.3 / 3.3 / 3.3; control oil 163.6 / 163.5 / 163.7; trip oil 163.7 / 163.6 / 163.2 kg/cm2; cooler inlet 62 °C, outlet 46 / 46 °C; generator bearings 55 / 56 °C. Pump running / stopped is read from the discharge pressure (snapshot note). The snapshot labels TILO1001 at the return; the TCS IO list says it is the cooler inlet oil temperature, which is used here.
NO-SOURCE (-----): pump AUTO / MANUAL mode, vapour extractor state, purifier state, Y / N of the pumps. The control valve TCV-CW1201 value 53.1 % is from another snapshot (27.PNG), so it is not shown on this page.
Not drawn: jacking oil and turning gear (Turbine Start page), seal oil of the generator, the pressure control valve of the lube oil, drains and vents.
