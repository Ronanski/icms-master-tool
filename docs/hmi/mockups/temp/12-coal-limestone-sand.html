Sources and flow check

Flow: traced on the drawing

Coal firing system (flow diagram set Rev A, page 48): coal conveyor (future) to coal bunkers T-1008A / B / C (LT-CL1008), manual knife valve, gravimetric feeders L-1061A / B / C (FT-CL1061, speed and load cells), outlet valve XV-CL1061 (ZSO / ZSC A7), 18 inch chute splitting to two fuel ports per feeder on the front wall (six in all); cold primary air from K1AU3-PD-052 as seal air to the feeder (FE-FA1061-A1) and chute (A2); hot primary air 28 inch from PD-052 to each fuel port (FE-FA1061-A3 / A4); plant air nozzles V-PA1101 to 1106 on the chutes.
Limestone feed system (pages 49 and 50): truck fill LM-1-822 / 823 to silo T-1311 (vent fan F-1313, vent filter S-1313, LT-LM1800, fluidizing XV-PA1813A / B), lock vessel inlet XV-LM1812 to lock vessel S-1311 (PT-LM1811), dispensing vessel inlet XV-LM1815 to dense phase vessel S-1312 (PT-LM1812), rotary feeder L-1311 with VFD, conveying line LM-1-812 5 inch to a 4 way static splitter, four 2 way splitters, isolating valves XV-LM1811A to F (front side) and G, H (rear side); bypass air XV-LM1812A / B from plant air PA-1-821; air tank T-1312 (plant air PA-1-058) feeds the vessel pressurising valves PV-PA1811 / PV-PA1812 and the burner A to D valve sets.
Sand injection system (pages 51 and 52): truck fill SD-1-810 and bottom ash recycle (PD-071) to silo T-1300 (vent fan F-1301, LT-SD1800), dome valve XV-SD1804 to dense phase vessel S-1300 (PT-SD1800), outlet valve XV-SD1801, SD-1-800 4 inch to a 2 way splitter, isolating valves XV-SD1802A / B to the furnace rear side, bypass air XV-PA1807; air tank T-1301 (plant air PA-1-059) for conveying air XV-PA1800, bypass XV-PA1805A, density stabiliser XV-PA1805B, fluidizing XV-PA1806A / B, vent XV-AV1803.
