Sources and flow check

Flow traced on the drawings

K1AU3-PD-023 COMPRESSED AIR SYSTEM, Rev C (flow-diagram zip, RA pages 23 to 28; no Rev D in the RB set). Sheet 2 (instrument air): each compressor C-0704A/B/C (motor M-0704A/B/C, lube oil Z-0704A/B/C, intake silencer Z-0712A/B/C, coolers and moisture traps in the package) discharges through check V-IA0001 / 0003 / 0005 and valve V-IA0002 / 0004 / 0006 into the 6 in header IA-0-013. The header feeds the three dryers through V-IA0007 / 0008 / 0009; each dryer has two pre-filters (S-0704A1 / A2) and two after-filters (A3 / A4) in parallel, PT-IA0107 / 0127 / 0147 at the outlet, then check V-IA0010 / 0011 / 0012 into header IA-0-020 / 021 to the tank inlets: tank B through V-IA0018, tank A through a valve drawn as "V-PA0013" on the IA sheet. Tank outlets: B through check V-IA0058 and V-IA0057, A through check V-IA0015 and V-IA0014, to the outlet header: Unit 1 (V-IA1101, IA-1-101 3 in, PT / PSL-IA1706), Unit 2 (V-IA2101), utility area (V-IA0059), compressor control air (V-IA0060, IA-0-024 2 in).
Sheet 1 (plant air): the same arrangement with C-0703A/B/C, Z-0710A/B/C, D-0703A/B/C, S-0703, V-PA0001 to 0012, tanks T-0705A/B 10 m3 (inlet V-PA0013 A, V-PA0029 B; outlet check V-PA0015 and V-PA0014 A, check V-PA0026 and V-PA0027 B), Unit 1 (V-PA1087, 6 in, PT / PSL-PA1705), Unit 2 (V-PA2087), utility area (V-PA0028). Control air: start-up compressor C-0707 through V-IA0021 and check V-IA0022 into header IA-0-031, which also takes the IA tank outlet (connector 7), feeds the IA compressor panels and PV-IA0001 to 0006, and through V-IA0016 and check V-IA0017 the plant air compressor panels (IA-0-044).
Users, sheets 3 to 6: plant air to the turbine room and boiler house hose points, start-up burner A to D oil guns, coal firing, sand injection, limestone feed, bottom ash, fly ash, oil separator pump; utility area (compressor room, emergency diesel generators, water treatment, water tank area, seawater pump station, fuel oil tank area, crusher room and coal yard). Instrument air to the T/G building manifolds, turning gear buffer tank T-1128, boiler house; utility area (diesel generator, crusher room and coal yard, CEMS room, water treatment, water storage tank inlet valve, fuel oil pump PCV-DO1001).

Tags, logic and limits

DCS IO list, STN105: MIR / MIT-C0704A/B/C, MOR-C0704A/B/C (DITL-113), IT-C0704A/B/C 0 to 500 A; MIR / MIT / MIV-C0703A/B/C, MOR-C0703A/B/C (DITL-114), IT-C0703A/B/C 0 to 140 A, TE-M0703 winding; dryers MIR / MIT (alarm) / MIL (remote), MOR / MOS (DITL-45), FI-D0703 0 to 5800, FI-D0704 0 to 2700 m3/h; PT-PA0705-A/B, PT-IA0706-A/B, PT-PA0107 / 0127 / 0147, PT-IA0107 / 0127 / 0147. STN104: PT-PA1705, PT-IA1706, PSL-PA1705, PSL-IA1706, PSL-IA1706-NC, PALL-IA1706 (to IRP), INS-PRESS-LL (from IRP).
Logic: DITL-113 / 114 start each standby compressor (not running, not tripped) with a 5 s pulse when two of tank A, tank B and Unit 1 pressure are below 5 kg/cm2 for 3 s. DITL-114 row 3 is written PT-PA1706; the IO list and PD-023 have PT-PA1705. DITL-45 (dryer start / stop) outputs are not readable in the app's logic text (NOTHING blocks); start / stop push buttons M.1110 to M.112B are listed.
Alarm list 2016-09-12: PT-PA0705-A/B and PT-PA1705 L 5.0, LL -1.0 (DCS values); PT-IA0706-A/B and PT-IA1706 L 5.0, LL 3.5; PSL-PA1705 2.8 alarm; PSL-IA1706 2.8 trip. PR-OPE-5002 sections 9 to 12: dryers before compressors, closed cooling water expansion tank at 1.7 m first, compressor start at the local panel.

Values and NO-SOURCE

NO-SOURCE: all values and states on this page (-----). The repo has no HMI snapshot of the air system (no 24 or 25 picture in docs/hmi/OPERATING_POINTS.md), so nothing is guessed.
No Y / N chip on the compressors: they are started at the local panel; the DCS only sends the auto start. The permissive faceplate lists the auto start and local start conditions.
Not drawn on this page: package internals (stages, intercoolers, traps, lube oil), the blow-off silencers Z-0711 / Z-0713, drains TRP, vents, the Unit 2 tie lines. PT-IA2706 and PT-PA2705 (Unit 2) are on the drawing but not in the Unit 1 IO list.
