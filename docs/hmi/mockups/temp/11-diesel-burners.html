Flow: traced on the drawing

K1AU3-PD-022 sheet 1 (Rev B, page 16): tank truck unloading through STR-DO0001 to oil service tank T-0701 (flame arrester Z-0701, LT-DO0701); outlet DO-0-004 through strainer STR-DO0002 (DPT-DO0001) to diesel oil pumps P-0702A / B; discharge DO-0-009 with recirculation PCV-DO0002 back to the tank; FCV-DO1012 to the burner header DO-1-035 and DO-1-025 / 027 / 036 / 037 to burners A to D; branch DO-2-021 to Unit 2; after FCV-DO1012 and check V-DO1030 the supply DO-1-021 feeds DO-1-023 (PT-DO1008, PG-DO1007), which rises to PCV-DO1009 (V-DO1023 in, V-DO1024 out, bypass V-DO1025) and returns to the tank by DO-1-024; supply relief ZV-DO1001 returns on DO-1-022 through V-DO1028 and check V-DO1027 into DO-1-024 after the PCV; burner relief valves ZV-DO1043A to D return on DO-1-028 / 029 / 040 / 041 through checks V-DO1033 to V-DO1036 into DO-1-042, which joins DO-1-024 after the PCV; oil return from Unit 2 DO-2-024.
K1AU3-PD-022 sheets 2 to 5 (pages 17 to 20, same layout): oil supply through V-DO1301, STR-DO1043A, flow meter FT-DO1043A, FCV-DO1043A3, shutoff valves XV-DO1043A1 and A2, check valve V-DO1302, PT / PSL at the gun, flexible hose DO-1-051 to oil gun L-1043A; atomizing air from the limestone feed PA tank (PA-1-083) through PRV-PA1043A1, PSL-PA1043A1, valve PA1043A2 and check V-PA1635 to the gun; purge valve from the air line into the oil line (check V-PA1671) downstream of the oil shutoff valves; hot secondary air from K1AU3-PD-053 through FT-FA1043-A and damper MV-FA1043A (ZT / ZSO / ZSC) to burner B-1043A; flame detector BT-DO1043A.
