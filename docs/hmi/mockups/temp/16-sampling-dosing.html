Sources and flow check

Flow: traced on the drawing

K1AU3-PD-044 Sampling, Analyzing and Monitoring System (flow diagram set Rev A, page 38): eight sample lines SP-1-001 deaerator upstream (PD-016), SP-1-002 deaerator outlet (PD-017), SP-1-003 make-up water pump outlet (PD-024), SP-1-004 economizer inlet (PD-019), SP-1-005 drum water blow down (PD-019), SP-1-006 saturated steam (PD-019), SP-1-007 main steam (PD-011), SP-1-008 condensate pump outlet (PD-016); each through coolers (cooling water PD-021, chiller unit Z-1110), pressure reducer, thermal shut-off valve, TG / PG-SP1001 to 1008, grab sample M1 to M8 to the common sink and the analyzers AIT-SP1001 to SP1020; silica analyzer panel streams A to D; drain WW-1-002 to PD-046.
K1AU3-PD-042 Chemical Dosing System (page 36): tri-sodium phosphate tank Z-1101 200 L with agitator, measuring vessel Z-1102 10 L, pumps Z-1107A / B (200 ml/min, 184.62 kg/cm2) on CH-1-001 to the boiler steam drum (economizer inlet, PD-017); neutral amine tank Z-1103 200 L, vessel Z-1104 15 L, pumps Z-1108A / B (250 ml/min, 18.36 kg/cm2) on CH-1-002 to the condensate pump inlet line and CH-1-003 to the condensate pump outlet after the polisher feedback point (PD-016); carbon hydraride tank Z-1105 100 L, vessel Z-1106 10 L, pumps Z-1109A / B (100 ml/min, 12.24 kg/cm2) on CH-1-004 to the deaerator (PD-016); demineralized water CH-1-032 (PD-016), drain to PD-040. The cycle strip at the top is a block summary of pages 2 and 5 to 8.

Values, logic and NO-SOURCE

Snapshot 21.PNG (021 SAMPLING SYSTEM, 149.9 MW, time not shown): every analyzer value on the cards; sampling system analyzer common alarm true (red check); AC main power abnormal, silica analyzer system alarm and chiller unit system alarm false.
