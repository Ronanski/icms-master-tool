Sources and flow check

Flow: traced on the drawing

Condensate system K1AU3-PD-016 Rev D (RB page 10). Hot well outlet CD-1-001 20 in (drain V-CD1083, neutral amine V-CH1001) to header CD-1-002. Each pump: suction valve V-CD1001 / 1002, strainer STR-CD1001 / 1002 with DPT-CD1001 / 1002, expansion joint, pump P-1116A/B, check V-CD1005 / 1006, discharge valve V-CD1009 / 1010, into CD-1-003 / 004 and the 10 in header CD-1-013 (PT / PS / PA-CD1117, TT-CD1118, sampling SP-1-008).
Then steam jet air ejector condenser H-1119 (V-CD1015 / 1016, bypass V-CD1017), TT-CD1119, gland steam condenser H-1903 (V-CD1018 / 1019, bypass V-CD1020), TT-CD1120, the 2 in branch CD-1-026 to the turbine exhaust hood spray, flow element FE / FT-CD1120. After it: flash chamber spray CD-1-037 3 in (XV-CD1004 normal path, XV-CD1005), polisher inlet CD-1-038 8 in (MV-CD1007, FT-CD1121), polisher bypass FCV-CD1121 (V-CD1070 / 1071, bypass V-CD1072), curtain spray CD-1-036 2 in (XV-CD1006), polisher return CD-1-039 8 in (MV-CD1008), neutral amine CH-1-003, desuperheater spray lines CD-1-027 / 028 4 in to the aux steam (XV-CD1123, TCV-CD1124), gland steam (XV-CD1125, TCV-CD1126) and LP turbine bypass (XV-CD1114, TCV-CD1115, FT-CD1115) desuperheaters.
The line then rises: chemical dosing CD-1-032 (V-CD1047), sampling SP-1-001 (V-SP1005), minimum flow FCV-CD1120 (V-CD1041 / 1042, bypass V-CD1043) by CD-1-029 6 in back to the condenser, deaerator initial fill MU-1-023 from make-up (V-MU1027, check V-MU1028), the dump CD-1-031 4 in through LCV-CD1110 (V-CD1044 / 1045, bypass V-CD1046) to connection 9 (Unit 2 condensate storage tank, PD-045; the IO list calls it "dumping to raw pit"), then LCV-CD1104 (V-CD1021 / 1022, bypass V-CD1023, check V-CD1024, V-CD1025) and CD-1-017 10 in down to the heaters.
Low pressure heaters in series: H-1101 (MV-CD1001 in, MV-CD1002 out, bypass MV-CD1003), H-1102 (V-CD1026 / 1027, bypass V-CD1028), H-1103 (V-CD1029 / 1030, bypass V-CD1031), then CD-1-025 10 in (FE / FT / PT / TT-CD1122, check V-CD1032, STR-CD1008) into deaerator H-1104 on storage tank T-1104 (LT-CD1104-1/2/3, LSLL-CD1104-4, emergency drain XV-CD1104-2 to the condenser). Make-up into the condenser: MV-MU1001 and LCV-MU1110. Polisher: K1AU3-PD-047 Rev C (RA page 41), a conceptual sheet: from the Unit 1 and Unit 2 condensate pumps through anthracite filters No.1 and No.2, mixed bed towers No.1 and No.2, booster pumps No.1 to No.4, back to each unit.

Logic. DITL-61A: start from the screen needs remote, not tripped, auto not selected and no hot well low-low; the pump in AUTO starts on outlet pressure low (other pump running 5 s), the other pump stopped with its trip condition or low pressure, or the other pump's strainer DP above 0.08 kg/cm2 (3 s); stop on hot well low-low (LSLL-CD1110 and ABC-031, 2 s), own strainer DP above 0.1 kg/cm2 (5 s), stop button (manual), trip condition or local stop. DITL-61B: trip condition from coupling 95 °C, bearing 100 °C, winding 165 °C, vibration 0.38 in/s (2 s); outlet pressure low below 14.0 kg/cm2 (1 s). DITL-63: XV-CD1004 opens in auto when a condensate pump runs; XV-CD1005 on flash chamber temperature high or load below 50 MW. DITL-62: curtain spray works with the LP bypass spray block valve XV-CD1114.
