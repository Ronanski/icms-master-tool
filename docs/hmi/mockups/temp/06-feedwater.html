DITL-23B: trip command on deaerator storage tank level low-low (LSLL-CD1104-4 or ABC-031) or lube oil pressure low-low (5 s); interlock condition on bearing, winding and vibration (5 s) and strainer DP (2 s). The comparator set points of the bearing, winding and vibration rows are not printed in the logic dump.
