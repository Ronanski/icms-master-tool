Sources and flow check

Flow traced on the drawings

Make-up water, K1AU3-PD-024 Rev D (flow-diagram zip, RB page 21): water from water treatment (CED1 permeate) through V-DW0892A/B into tanks T-0708A/B; tank outlets V-MU0001 / V-MU0002 to the 12 in suction header MU-0-003; each pump: suction valve V-MU0003/4/5, strainer STR-MU0001/2/3 (DPT-MU0709-A/B/C across it), pump P-0709A/B/C, then the minimum-flow line (orifice, check V-MU0012/14/16, valve V-MU0013/15/17) back to the tanks, then check V-MU0006/8/10 and discharge valve V-MU0007/9/11 into the 12 in header MU-0-010 with PT-MU0709. Header splits to Unit 2 (MU-2-020, 12 in) and Unit 1 (MU-1-020, 12 in): sampling (PD-044, V-SP1011), boiler initial fill (PD-017), deaerator initial fill (PD-016), condenser make-up (PD-016), expansion tank (PD-021, MU-1-021, 3 in).
Closed cooling water, K1AU3-PD-021 Rev D (RB page 15): make-up 3 in through V-MU1031, XV-MU1109, V-MU1032 (bypass V-MU1033) into expansion tank T-1109; tank bottom line CW-1-001 8 in through V-CW1001 to the pump suction line CW-1-003 (two-way arrow on the drawing), where the users' return CW-1-002 20 in also joins. Each pump: suction valve V-CW1002 / V-CW1004, strainer STR-CW1001 / 1002 (DPT-CW1108-A/B), pump P-1108A/B, check V-CW1006 / V-CW1008, discharge valve V-CW1007 / V-CW1009, into the 20 in discharge CW-1-007 (PT / TT-CW1152). Heat exchangers H-1107A/B/C in parallel: inlet V-CW1010 / 1011 / 1012, outlet V-CW1013 / 1014 / 1015 (TG-CW1107-A/B/C), into the 20 in supply CW-1-014 (PT / TT-CW1153), then to the users and back on the return line.
Users on PD-021 (the same list as PR-OPE-5002 section 8 "ensure cooling water was established to"): turbine oil coolers A/B with TCV-CW1201, generator air coolers A to D, boiler feed pumps P-1115A/B (motor cooler, pump, lube oil cooler Z-1115A/B), bottom ash screw conveyors L-1060A to D, PA fan oil cooler Z-1053, SA fan bearings F-1054, gas air heater H-1052, ID fan oil cooler Z-1056 and bearings F-1056, instrument air compressors C-0704A/B/C and dryers D-0704A/B/C, plant air compressors C-0703A/B/C and dryers D-0703A/B/C (with a tie to the Unit 2 closed cooling water), sampling rack. On this page the users are grouped as a ladder; the exact branch lines of each user will be on the system page that owns it.

Logic. DITL-112 make-up pump manual start: remote, not tripped, not in auto, not both tanks below 0.2 m (2 s), strainer DP not at 1 kg/cm2 or more (10 s); header pressure low-low starts the standby pump. DITL-77 closed cooling pump B: manual start needs remote, not tripped, auto not selected; auto start from pump A trip, pump A trip condition (M.1234) or discharge pressure low (M.250D, 5 s); trip on bearing above 100 °C or winding above 165 °C (2 s), strainer DP above 0.8 kg/cm2 (5 s), expansion tank below 0.3 m. DITL-78 fill valve XV-MU1109 in auto: opens below 40 % and closes above 85 % of LT-CW1109.
Alarm list 2016-09-12: LT-MU0708 normal 13.8, L 2, LL 0.2 m; PT-MU0709 H 9.2, normal 7.0, L 6.5, LL 6.0 (LL starts the standby pump); PT-CW1152 H 7.0, L 5.3, normal 4.5; PT-CW1153 H 7.0, L 5.0, LL 4.8, normal 3.9; TE-CW1152 H 60; TE-CW1153 H 40; LT-CW1109 H 1700, L 800, LL 300 mm; DPT-CW1108 H 0.6, HH 0.8; motor bearing H 95, HH 100 °C; winding H 155, HH 165 °C. PR-OPE-5002 sections 7 and 8: tank above 2 m, expansion tank normal 1.7 m, header above 6.0 kg/cm2.
