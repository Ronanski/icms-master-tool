Sources and flow check

Logic read

DITL-01: FURNACE PURGE PERMISSIVE / SATISFIED = AND of PAF running (M.3302), SAF running (M.3303), any FAB running (M.330F), IDF running (M.3304), no flame on burners A to D, oil valves A to D all closed, NOT limestone rotary valve running, coal feeder A / B / C outlet valves closed (I.0125 / 0445 / 0485) and feeders stopped, no boiler trip command (I.0561), RH damper at least 30 % (M.0506), SH damper at least 60 % (M.0507), furnace purge permissive from 03A (cyclone outlet and reheater outlet temperature not high-high), NOT total air flow below 25 % MCR (M.3100).
DITL-02: burner A to D secondary air dampers at the 25 % purge position (M.3126 / 3127 / 3146 / 3147), start push button M.0500, 300 s on-delay while the permissive stays, purge completed to 03A-21, 03F-15 and ABC-020; purge bypass when the furnace lower layer is above 700 °C (M.3339, ABC-027): completed 7 s after MFT with no trip command.
DITL-03A: MFT flip-flop set by any of 18 IRP inputs (IDF / SAF / PAF stopped, all FAB stopped (60 s on-delay, DITL-03B), total air below 25 % MCR, furnace pressure high-high (2 of 3, 5 s, DITL-03C) and low-low (03D), drum level high-high and low-low (03E), loss of all fuel, primary air flow low-low, furnace temperature inadequate for coal firing, emergency trip push buttons on the screen and the desk (03F), instrument air low-low (03G), cyclone outlet gas temp high-high with no reheater steam, reheater outlet header temp high-high, turbine trip (03H)); reset by purge completed with no input left. Outputs: NO BOILER TRIP COMMAND I.0561, MFT I.0560, MFT 1 / MFT 2 to TCS, SER, OPC.
