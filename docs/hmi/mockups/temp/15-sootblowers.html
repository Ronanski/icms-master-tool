Sources and flow check

Flow: traced on the drawing

K1AU3-PD-015 Steam Soot Blowing System (flow diagram set Rev A, page 11; no Rev B sheet): steam from the S.H. panel 1 outlet (PD-019) on SB-1-002 2 in through motor valve MV-SB1001 and pressure control valve PCV-SB1053 (PIC-SB1053) into header SB-1-003 4 in (PG-SB1051, PT / PI-SB1052); relief ZV-SB1001 on SB-1-101 10 in to the silencer (PD-019).
The header feeds riser SB-1-004 3 in down the middle between the passes (blowers N-1012, 1016 ... 1036 on the reheater pass and N-1014, 1018 ... 1038 on the superheater pass) and riser SB-1-005 3 in on the right (blowers N-1013, 1017 ... 1037, the left wall blowers N-1011, 1015 ... 1035 through branches SB-1-007 to 031 and connectors 1 to 7, and the air heater blowers N-1040 hot end, N-1042 cold end). Location table on the sheet: RH pass N-1011 / 1012 ... 1035 / 1036, SH pass N-1013 / 1014 ... 1037 / 1038.
Drains: the middle riser through SB-1-046, TE / TT / TI-SB1056, TG-SB1055, trap TRP-SB1003 (V-SB1508 / 1509) to SB-1-043, check V-SB1510 to the blowdown tank (PD-019), bypass SB-1-044 / 045 through XV-SB1054 (V-SB1004 / 1005); the right riser through SB-1-039, TE / TT / TI-SB1057, TG-SB1058, trap TRP-SB1002 (V-SB1505 / 1506) to SB-1-040, check V-SB1507 to the blowdown tank, bypass SB-1-041 / 042 through XV-SB1059 (V-SB1002 / 1003).

Logic DITL-80A: blowing permissive M.400A = drain temperature A and B above 220 °C and steam supply valve open for 600 s; drain valves auto close when both drain temperatures are above 250 °C after 600 s; N-1011 operating = start command and not skipped and blowing permissive. DITL-80: sequence counter, program reset, auto start mode, all retracted M.4008, any skipped M.4195. PR-OPE-5002: soot blowing in start-up condition, all blowers retracted, steam supply isolation open; air heater blowers on plant air during start-up.
